PrintFarm Capture

In a group, select a post's text, right-click, and choose Capture post to PrintFarm. Only that text, the page title (as the group's name) and the post's link are sent, and only when you choose Capture. Replies are drafted on the dashboard; you post them yourself.

If the test says the dashboard cannot be reached, this device may not trust the dashboard's certificate yet (README step 7).
